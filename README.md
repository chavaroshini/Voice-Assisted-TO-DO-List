## future Implementation

1). Natural Language Understanding – Interpret complex voice commands like “Remind me every Friday at 7 PM to take medicine.”

2). Smart Scheduling Integration – Auto-detect time/date from voice and sync with Google Calendar or local reminders.

3). Voice-Based Notifications – Use text-to-speech to announce tasks and accept voice responses like “mark as done” or “snooze.”

4). Recurring Tasks by Voice – Support for setting repeating tasks hands-free, e.g., “Repeat this every Monday.”

5). AI-Powered Suggestions – Recommend tasks or remind based on usage patterns and voice history.
